OUR STORY

Built On Charcoal, Spice & Patience

Flame & Sizzle started as a single charcoal drum on a street corner. Twelve years on, we still cook the same way: meat marinated overnight, spice rubs blended in-house every morning, and nothing touching the grill that we would not serve our own family.

Our shawarma spits turn for hours so the edges caramelize. Our potatoes are hand-cut and double-fried to order. Our sauces are made fresh daily — never from a jar.

  • ✔ Overnight marinated, never frozen meats
  • ✔ Halal-certified supply chain
  • ✔ Zero artificial flavour enhancers
  • ✔ Locally sourced produce, delivered daily

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about delivery, dietary needs and ordering.

How long does delivery usually take?

Most orders reach you in 20–30 minutes within a 10-mile radius. Peak hours (7–9 PM Fri–Sun) may add 10 minutes. You can follow every stage in the Live Order Tracker.

Is your meat halal certified?

Yes. Our beef, lamb and chicken come from halal-certified suppliers. Items containing bacon or pork are clearly flagged and can be hidden using the “100% Halal Certified” filter on the menu.

Do you cater for large groups and events?

We do. Platters start at 10 guests and scale to 300. Call our hotline at least 48 hours ahead and we will build a custom grill menu around your budget.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, mobile transfer, and cash on delivery. Card payments are processed over an encrypted connection and we never store your card details.

Can I customise spice levels and add-ons?

Every item has a “Customize” button — choose portion size, complimentary sauces, paid add-ons, and leave special instructions for the kitchen.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Call the hotline within 30 minutes of delivery. We will remake the item or refund it in full, no questions asked.
